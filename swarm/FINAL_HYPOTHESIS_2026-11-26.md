## The dream path, in a few lines

The deep dream's survivor 1 (the three-leg abstention audit) was
an instrument, not a claim: it said how rivals would be measured,
not what the measurement would find. The other survivors each
priced one way a single-number abstention claim fails: drift
expires a calibration fit (D3), trigger rankings can flip by
insufficiency slice (D2), side signals enter decisions untested
(D5), and probe evidence itself can postdate the claim it judges
(D4). The convergence: all five are the same failure seen from
different sides. A headline number in this benchmark family is
reported at one operating point, on one evidence state, with the
abstentions invisible. The study's own record proves the pattern
twice from the inside (the Stage B pristine collapse, decided
fraction 0.9800 against 0.4833 with falsified detection at zero;
the Arm W mirror artifact). The dream therefore converged on the
three-leg accounting as the core, sharpened from an audit
procedure into a falsifiable prediction about other people's
numbers: that the pattern found inside this study is also present,
unreported, in at least one named rival's published gain. D3 and
D2 survive as the two supporting sub-hypotheses, because each is
the same claim stressed along one axis (time, and evidence type).
D4 and D5 stay as registered avenues; they support the programme
but are not legs of this hypothesis.

## The hypothesis as first stated (v1)

"On MMFakeBench, NewsCLIPpings, and VERITE, at least one named
published system's reported gain is materially an abstention
movement: under three-leg restatement (decided fraction,
lost-correct count against its own no-abstention read, and
delivery of its stated target risk), its headline advantage
shrinks materially or changes character, and the benchmark
family's recoverability rate is below 100 percent."

Supporting sub-hypotheses as first stated:
S1. A temperature fitted on one evidence snapshot fails to
deliver its stated target risk on drifted evidence for the same
items, beyond the re-judging noise floor, and a split-half refit
on the drifted half restores delivery.
S2. The ranking of abstention triggers in the sufficiency race
reverses between the absent-evidence and contradictory-evidence
slices of the frozen probe.

## The board attacks, in short

**Novelty critic.** The three legs are each owned in their home
settings: selective prediction theory is Chow and Geifman and
El-Yaniv; lost-correct accounting is ReFrain's, in QA; the
stated-target delivery check is Entropy Alone's, in short-form
QA; selective fact-checking restatement exists as self-reporting
(Evidence Chain Evaluation, arXiv 2607.18240). What no pass has
found occupied is the composed outward instrument on these three
benchmarks, and that verdict carries the standing limit of both
searches: Semantic Scholar coverage was never checked, so the
absence claim is "not found in the searches run," not proven
absent. The critic's sharper attack: v1's "at least one system"
is an existential claim so weak it is nearly free to confirm and
hard to kill, the same shape as the pattern-hunting this study
criticises in others. A hypothesis that any single failure
confirms is not a prediction; it is a fishing licence.

**Methods critic.** "Shrinks materially" and "changes character"
are undefined, and an undefined margin lets the audit declare
victory at any size. Margins must be registered before any file
is opened. Second: rivals' operating points are often fitted on
the split they are reported on (the D6 tag, folded into N1);
restatement must take published outputs as released and never
refit a rival's threshold, or the audit commits the fitted-read
sin this study's own scorer was caught in (finding 77d29bf1).
Third: the closed MMFakeBench test set binds this study's own
new reads, not the reading of rivals' already-published outputs;
the protocol must say so explicitly so the leg is not later
misread as a test-set violation. Fourth: leg 2 (lost-correct)
needs a no-abstention read. Almost no rival publishes one. As
stated, leg 2 is computable only where per-item outputs include
the pre-abstention verdict or a forced-choice score; everywhere
else it is not a failed leg, it is a missing one, and v1 did not
distinguish the two.

**Statistics critic.** Decided fractions and lost-correct counts
computed from released per-item outputs are exact counts, not
estimates; no interval is needed and none should be theatrical.
But any leg that requires fresh judging by this study (the own-
systems demonstration, S1's drift re-judging) inherits the
re-judging noise floor of 2.1 to 2.4 percent (finding 743f3fce),
and its margins must clear that floor or the leg reads noise.
S2's slices hold roughly a hundred probe items each; a ranking
reversal whose paired resampling intervals fully overlap is a
descriptive observation, not a confirmation, and v1 did not say
so. The recoverability rate needs its denominator frozen in
advance: the named-system list from record section 2.4 plus the
review's comparator set, fixed at registration, not assembled
from whoever replies.

**Feasibility critic.** The gate is unchanged and it is not
money: rivals' per-item predictions are not in hand for any
system. v1 could therefore be "confirmed" trivially on one
friendly file or left untestable forever, and both outcomes are
worthless. The hypothesis needs a coverage floor written into
itself: a minimum number of external systems across a minimum
number of benchmarks, obtained inside one registered acquisition
window (public files first, then author requests with a logged
deadline), below which the outward claim is declared untestable
rather than quietly shrunk to fit whoever replied. The
own-systems leg (LAYA's arms restated in the three-leg format,
$0, artifacts in hand) is a methods demonstration that the
instrument detects the pattern where it is known to exist; it is
not evidence for the outward claim and must not be counted as
any part of confirmation. S1 waits on the same network window
H-C already needs; S2's judging passes fit inside the $3 swarm
cap or run at no marginal cost on the pending A100. Neither
sub-hypothesis needs a new approval.

## Revisions forced by the board

R1 (novelty and methods critics): the existential claim is
replaced by registered survival margins. A system survives
restatement only if every recoverable leg passes: decided
fraction within 2 points of its comparator's; lost-correct count
under 1 percent of items; stated target risk delivered inside
its paired resampling interval where a target is stated. One
failed recoverable leg fails the system.
R2 (methods critic): a leg that cannot be computed from released
outputs is marked not recoverable. It counts toward the
recoverability rate and never toward survival or failure.
R3 (methods critic): published outputs are restated as released.
No rival threshold is refit. Reading rivals' published outputs
on any split, including MMFakeBench test, is permitted; this
study's own new reads stay on validation, tune, probe, and
held-out splits.
R4 (statistics critic): margins on any leg requiring fresh
judging by this study must exceed the re-judging noise floor of
2.1 to 2.4 percent. S2 confirms only if the reversed pair's
paired resampling interval on the slice difference excludes
zero; a bare point-estimate flip is recorded as descriptive.
R5 (feasibility critic): a coverage floor is written into the
hypothesis. Confirmation requires at least three external
systems across at least two of the three benchmarks, obtained
inside one registered acquisition window. Below the floor, the
hypothesis is untestable as an outward claim and dies as the
lead (kill condition K2 below), whatever the own-systems table
shows.
R6 (feasibility critic): the denominator of the recoverability
rate is the named-system list frozen at registration (record
section 2.4 systems plus the literature review's comparator
set). The rate is reported per leg.

## The final hypothesis (v2, as revised under fire)

"For the named published systems on MMFakeBench, NewsCLIPpings,
and VERITE whose per-item outputs can be obtained inside one
registered acquisition window, abstention is doing unreported
work. A system survives three-leg restatement only if all of its
recoverable legs pass: (1) its decided fraction sits within 2
points of its comparator's on the same split; (2) its
lost-correct count against its own no-abstention read is under
1 percent of items; (3) where it states a target risk, its
delivered selective risk meets that target inside its paired
resampling interval. The hypothesis predicts that at least one
obtainable system fails at least one recoverable leg, and that
recoverability itself is partial: leg 1 recoverable for under
100 percent of the frozen named-system list, and leg 2 for under
50 percent, because almost no rival releases a no-abstention
read. Legs that cannot be computed from released outputs are
marked not recoverable and count toward the recoverability rate
only. Published outputs are restated as released; no rival
operating point is refit."

Supporting sub-hypotheses (final form):
S1, calibration expiry under drift. A temperature fitted on the
frozen snapshot fails to deliver its stated target risk on
one-day-drifted evidence for the same items, with the delivery
gap exceeding the re-judging noise floor of 2.1 to 2.4 percent,
and a split-half refit on the drifted half restores delivery.
Killed by: the snapshot fit holding on drifted evidence, which
instead hands record claim 3 a stated boundary.
S2, trigger ranking flips by insufficiency slice. In the
three-way sufficiency trigger race (disagreement, sufficiency
probability, representation probe) run on the frozen
NewsCLIPpings probe under per-slice lost-correct budgets, at
least one pairwise ranking reverses between the absent-evidence
slice (refused at 86.4 percent in MM-AQA's read) and the
contradictory-evidence slice (37.6 percent), with the reversed
pair's paired resampling interval on the slice difference
excluding zero. Killed by: identical rankings in every slice,
which leaves the pooled race standing.

Confirmation legs for the main hypothesis:
L0, instrument check ($0, artifacts in hand, not evidence for
the outward claim): this study's own arms restated in the
three-leg format on all three benchmarks reproduce the two
known collapses (Stage B pristine, Arm W mirror). If the
instrument cannot detect the pattern where it is known to
exist, the hypothesis is not tested and the instrument is
repaired first.
L1, outward core: at least three external systems across at
least two benchmarks restated from obtained per-item outputs;
at least one fails at least one recoverable leg at the
registered margins.
L2, recoverability: the per-leg recoverability rates over the
frozen named-system list come out under the predicted ceilings
(leg 1 under 100 percent, leg 2 under 50 percent).

Kill conditions:
K1, tested and false: every obtainable external system passes
every recoverable leg at the registered margins. The outward
pattern does not exist at these margins and the lead dies on
evidence.
K2, untestable: fewer than three external systems across fewer
than two benchmarks are obtainable when the registered
acquisition window closes. The hypothesis dies as the lead
claim; the recoverability census folds into N1, the provenance
restatement (avenue e2f7f728), which becomes the lead candidate
in its place, and the three-leg format survives only as this
study's self-reporting standard.

## Verdict

**SURVIVES REVISED.** The final wording is v2 above, with
sub-hypotheses S1 and S2 in their revised forms. The hypothesis
remains the standing lead (H-A) for paper two, now as a
prediction with registered margins, a coverage floor, and two
distinct deaths (false, or untestable) instead of an audit
procedure that could only succeed. The single strongest attack
was the feasibility critic's, backed by the novelty critic:
as first stated, the hypothesis could be confirmed by one
friendly file or left untestable forever, and its lost-correct
leg was computable for almost no rival. That attack forced
R1, R2, R5, and R6: survival margins, the not-recoverable
marking, the coverage floor, and the frozen denominator, which
together converted the audit into a claim that can lose.
