## The candidates

**E1. The sufficiency race gains a fourth arm and a date axis.**
The registered race (N5, avenue f874e11b) and its sliced form
(D2, avenue cd9428b4) compare three outcome-derived triggers.
HumblePRM (finding 35206af8) shows a fourth competitor class: a
separately supervised sufficiency step raised ambiguous-item
abstention from 48.34 to 61.55 where outcome supervision left it
flat, at about 0.6 points of clear-item accuracy. VeriTaS
(finding fec43c32) shows static benchmark claims leak into
pretraining corpora, so a race run on old items may be measuring
memorised sufficiency rather than judgement. The date-split probe
(avenue 03beef00) tests one step's survival on later claims, but
nobody has run the race itself split by claim date. The cluster
runs the four-way race (disagreement, sufficiency probability,
representation probe, supervised sufficiency step) on the frozen
probe, scored per insufficiency slice as in D2 and per date side
of the model's training window. Killed by: the ranking coming
out identical on both date sides and the supervised arm failing
to beat the best outcome-derived trigger on the ambiguous slice,
in which case D2 stands as registered and the date axis folds
into 03beef00. Cost: one small supervised step trained on
validation items only; inference shared with the N5 and H-H
setup, $0 marginal on the A100 or inside the cap. Datasets:
NewsCLIPpings frozen probe, VERITE held-out as second read.
Validation, probe, and held-out splits only.

**E2. The escalation leg: routing is abstention by another
name.** MAGIC-3 (finding c816fc40) never abstains; it routes
uncertain items to a stronger model and keeps them inside the
headline accuracy, reporting routing as a cost mechanism, not
as coverage accounting. The final hypothesis counts decided
fraction, but a routed item is decided by a different and more
expensive system than the one the headline names. The cluster
(H-A three legs, avenue ddee4bdc; ReFrain's lost-correct
accounting, finding 8a61539e; the composition audit, avenue
36456354) adds a fourth restatement quantity: the escalation
fraction, the share of headline items whose final verdict came
from a fallback or stronger stage, plus the first stage's own
accuracy on the routed items where outputs allow. At the board
this was narrowed: the leg is computed from what papers
themselves document about their pipelines (a reporting fact),
not from per-item outputs, so it is recoverable at desk level
for documented systems and marked not recoverable otherwise,
under the hypothesis's own rule R2. Killed by: no obtainable
system documenting an escalation stage, or every documented
escalation fraction sitting under the leg 1 margin of 2 points.
Cost $0 desk. Datasets: all three benchmarks, published reports
and validation splits. THIS CANDIDATE PROPOSES A CHANGE TO THE
FINAL HYPOTHESIS; see the flagged section at the end.

**E3. The evidence hygiene gate in front of the three legs.**
GLAD (finding 8b3fca32) shows a small contaminated share of
retrieved evidence can dominate top-k results, and that defences
tested only on crude attacks fail on fluent, similarity-matched
contamination. TeViD (finding ee5534e9) treats leakage exclusion
as a design fact. The temporal validity audit (D4, avenue
fd744bd3) counts postdated evidence in this study's own probe
sets; the stage-separated contamination audit (avenue 1d1af268)
separates contamination by pipeline stage in this study's own
pipeline. The outward form is unregistered: before any rival is
restated under the three legs, its evidence condition is graded
per stage from its own paper (retrieval contamination exposure,
temporal postdating, pretraining leakage of the benchmark
claims), and the restatement is reported with that grade
attached. A system whose gain sits on evidence that fails the
gate is reported as failing hygiene before its legs are read.
Killed by: hygiene grades being unassignable from published
papers for most of the frozen named-system list, in which case
the gate folds into the recoverability census as one more not
recoverable marking. Cost $0 desk. Datasets: all three
benchmarks, published reports only.

**E4. Evidence coverage expiry, the sibling of S1.** S1 prices
the expiry of a verdict temperature under drift. Two-Sided
Conformal Evidence Sets (finding 55e2d455) show the evidence set
carries its own calibrated guarantee, inclusion coverage, which
holds under exchangeability and breaks measurably under shift.
DriftBench (finding 8d5cf345) shows evidence-level drift is a
separable failure level costing 14.8 percent F1 on its own. The
cluster asks whether the evidence set's stated coverage expires
on the same drift window where S1 tests the temperature: same
items, frozen snapshot evidence against one-day-drifted
evidence, coverage read against the re-judging noise floor.
Killed by: snapshot evidence coverage holding on drifted
evidence inside the floor, which hands the evidence layer a
stated boundary instead. Cost: rides the same network window
S1 and H-C already need; judging passes inside the cap.
Datasets: NewsCLIPpings probe and VERITE held-out. Validation,
probe, and held-out splits only.

**E5. The leg 3 provenance condition.** Leg 3 of the final
hypothesis checks delivered selective risk against a stated
target, but takes the target at face value. Three new findings
say the target itself has a provenance: Srivastava (finding
94054874) shows coverage validity hinges on which fold supplied
the calibration data, not on the abstention rule; SC-LEC
(finding a7e63be5) proves a largest-feasible fitted threshold
can violate the very target it was fitted to meet; Singh
(finding d2f21006) shows a risk guarantee does not transfer
across tasks even when the score stays informative. The cluster
adds a marking rule to leg 3: a delivery claim counts as
registered only if its target was certified on calibration data
disjoint from the reported split with a controlled threshold
rule; a largest-feasible fit on the reported split is marked
fitted, and a fitted delivery claim cannot carry a system to
survival on leg 3. Killed by: no obtainable system stating a
target risk at all (leg 3 stays mostly not recoverable and the
condition is moot), or every stated target turning out
registered, in which case the condition costs nothing and
changes nothing. Cost $0 desk. Datasets: all three benchmarks,
published reports. THIS CANDIDATE PROPOSES A CHANGE TO THE
FINAL HYPOTHESIS; see the flagged section at the end.

**E6. A leg 2 shadow from score and verdict divergence.** Shah
(finding 79e41373) shows judge scores lagging verdict accuracy
under corrupted evidence. The candidate proposed reading the
divergence between a system's score-implied verdict and its
stated verdict, under evidence perturbation, as a partial
stand-in for leg 2 (lost-correct) for systems that release
scores and verdicts but no forced-choice read.

**E7. Disagreement-stratified scoring inside the race.** Azizi
(finding a3b9c4eb) stratifies calibration by a disagreement
score fixed before calibration; Janga (finding 335d0bc5) shows
pooled calibration failing on slices. The candidate proposed
adding a per-stratum calibration delivery condition to the
sufficiency race's win rule, inside each insufficiency slice.

**E8. The abstention allocation audit.** Srivastava's second
result (finding 94054874) says the abstention score tracks
local label variance more than novelty. The candidate proposed
scoring each race trigger by how much of its abstention budget
lands on high label variance items rather than genuinely
insufficient ones.

## The board, in short

Novelty critic. E1 is the first race design the pooled and
sliced races cannot absorb: neither has a supervised arm, and
neither has a date axis; HumblePRM owns the supervised step in
its home setting, VeriTaS owns the leakage observation, but the
race built from both is unoccupied. E2's escalation fraction is
known as a cost number in the cascade literature; as a
restatement leg on these benchmarks, with the first stage's
routed-item accuracy beside it, it is not occupied in the
record. E3 is D4 and 1d1af268 turned outward; the outward turn
is the new part, and the critic accepts it only with the grade
reported as a reporting fact, never as a measured quantity. E4
is a genuine sibling of S1, not a restatement of it: the
temperature and the evidence set are different objects with
different guarantees. E5 walks close to the D6 grave: operating
point provenance was killed as a standalone avenue and folded
into N1 as a tag. The critic lets E5 stand only because it is
not a tag but a pass condition inside leg 3, and only as a
flagged proposed change, never merged quietly.

Methods critic. E6 is the fitted-read sin in a new coat: a
divergence proxy is not the lost-correct count, and reporting
it beside leg 2 invites exactly the misreading the hypothesis's
rule R2 exists to prevent. A leg that cannot be computed is
marked not recoverable; it is not shadowed. E6 dies here. E7 is
real and belongs inside the sliced race's protocol as a scoring
note, the way the sufficiency label ceiling (D7 of the first
dream) became a note inside N5; as a standalone avenue it
duplicates D2's ground. E5's marking rule is the study's own
fitted versus registered discipline turned outward, consistent
with revision R3 (restate as released, never refit), because
the rule judges the claim's provenance, not a refitted number.
E1's supervised arm must be trained on validation items only,
with its training fold stated, or the race imports the fitted
problem it studies. E2 survives only in its narrowed form:
documented routing from papers, not inferred routing.

Statistics critic. E8 dies on arithmetic: the frozen probe
holds about 300 items, its insufficiency slices about 100 each,
and label variance strata inside those slices leave cells too
thin for any paired resampling interval to exclude zero; the
question is real and the instrument is too small. E1's date
axis halves the same probe; the registered falsifier (identical
rankings on both date sides) is what keeps it honest, and any
reversal claim needs its interval on the slice difference to
exclude zero, per revision R4. E4's coverage reads inherit the
re-judging noise floor of 2.1 to 2.4 percent; the paired
same-item design carries it. E2 and E3 and E5 are counts and
reporting facts over a frozen denominator, so no interval is
needed and none should be performed.

Feasibility critic. E2, E3, and E5 start tonight at $0 on
published papers; their recoverability risk is the hypothesis's
own subject, so a low recovery rate is a result, not a failure
of the dream. E1 needs one small training run (a sufficiency
step on validation items), which fits the pending A100 or the
cap, and shares all judging passes with N5 and H-H. E4 waits on
the same single network window S1 and H-C already need; it asks
for no new approval. Nothing in this pass touches the closed
MMFakeBench test set.

## Flagged: proposed changes to the final hypothesis

These two survivors would change the final hypothesis
(finding f0d2d45b, verdict SURVIVES REVISED, 2026-11-26). They
are recorded here and in their avenue rows as PROPOSED CHANGES
ONLY. The hypothesis wording stands as registered until the
owner orders a board review of the changes themselves.

- **Change 1 (from E2): add a fourth leg, escalation.** A system
  survives restatement only if its recoverable legs pass,
  where leg 4 reads: the share of headline items decided by a
  fallback or stronger stage, as documented in the system's own
  report, sits within the leg 1 margin of its comparator's, and
  routed items are excluded from any decided fraction claimed
  for the first stage alone. Legs that cannot be documented are
  marked not recoverable under R2.
- **Change 2 (from E5): leg 3 gains a provenance condition.** A
  stated target risk counts toward survival on leg 3 only if it
  was certified on calibration data disjoint from the reported
  split under a controlled threshold rule. A target fitted
  largest-feasible on the reported split is marked fitted, and
  a fitted delivery claim cannot carry a system to survival.
